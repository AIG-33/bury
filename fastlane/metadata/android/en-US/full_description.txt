PlayTennis helps you organize the game of tennis — wherever you play. Find a sparring partner, pick a coach, join tournaments or organize your own — in a couple of taps, without group chats or Excel spreadsheets. The app is free.

FIND A SPARRING PARTNER NEARBY
Filter players by neighborhood, convenient time and skill level. Tap "Propose a match", agree on a time and get on court. No endless back-and-forth in group chats.

TOURNAMENTS YOU CAN RUN YOURSELF
Join open tournaments or launch your own in five minutes. Six formats to choose from: Single Elimination, Round Robin, Swiss, Group + Playoff and more. The wizard sets up the draw, schedule and scoring rules for you.

COACHES YOU CAN TRUST
Coach profiles with schedule, price and court address. Only players with a genuinely confirmed lesson can leave a review — no fake ratings.

RATING AND PROGRESS
Your level updates automatically after every match. It's handy for setting goals and tracking your growth — but it's a bonus, not a requirement. Play for fun.

WHY PLAYTENNIS
• Open platform — tournaments and matches are visible to everyone.
• Fast sign-in with Google, Apple or email.
• Courts, coaches and players in your city — the community grows every day.
• Completely free.
